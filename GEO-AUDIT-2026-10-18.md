## Executive Summary

**Overall GEO Score: 65/100 (Fair)**

The site has roughly quadrupled since August — 69 pages and 80,833 words against 24 and 21,669 — and the new material is better built than the old. `/regulatory` is the standout: 17 instruments, every one carrying an explicit "does not cover" statement and a visible *Verified 31 August 2026*, with `Legislation` and `CreativeWork` correctly split. Two things hold the score where it is, and they are different in kind.

The first is **off-domain, and it has not moved at all in eight weeks**: zero independent editorial mentions, zero directory listings, no Wikidata item. Worth stating plainly because it cuts against the Brand score: CamFinTech's `/learn` pages **are** being cited by answer engines on unbranded licensing queries, alongside Acclime and Lexology. Citability is working ahead of authority, which is the right order for a pre-revenue firm.

## Critical — fix before anything else on this list

### C1. `/learn/fintech-license-cambodia` publishes NBC capital thresholds — a class the project prohibits site-wide, unsourced, and an answer engine is already contesting them

**What is live.** A full table plus prose restating it:

| Licence type | Figure published |
|---|---|
| Payment Service Provider | **$500,000** |
| E-Money Issuer | **$1,500,000** |
| Microfinance Institution | **$1,500,000** |
| Deposit-taking MFI | **$7,500,000** |
| Specialized Bank | **$15,000,000** |

The page carries **zero outbound citations**. Nothing on it traces any figure to an issuer or a law firm.

**Why this is Critical rather than High.** `CLAUDE.md` states the rule without qualification: *"Never publish a capital threshold, licence tier or fee from a secondary aggregator. Those figures were the least reliable part of the source research and are excluded site-wide until an issuer or a law firm states them."* This page is that exact prohibited class, in a table, with a licence-tier breakdown.

**And it is not sitting quietly.** The brand sweep found an answer engine attributing *"$500,000 for PSPs and $1.5 million for e-money issuers, citing a 2023 NBC Prakas"* to this URL — and in one case **flagging it as conflicting with another source** (Kaadxpay, USD 2–5M) with a *"verify the Prakas before relying on these"* caveat. The figures are being read, attributed, and publicly disputed.

This is also the page the same sweep found **ranking on unbranded licensing queries alongside Acclime and Lexology** — so it is simultaneously the best-performing and the most exposed page on the domain.

**Fix — same day.** Either source every figure to a fetched primary (NBC Prakas text) and record the verification date the way `/regulatory` does, or remove the table and the prose figures. Do not leave them up while sourcing. The `/regulatory` evidence bar already describes the correct standard; this page predates it and was never brought up to it.

**Related, and checked:** the sweep also saw an engine attribute a *"45 locations retail group"* case study to `/use-cases/enterprise-bakong-integration`. **That text is not on the live page** — the 2026-08-31 rewrite removed it and the rewrite held. It is a stale index artifact from the fabricated originals, which is its own finding (H-E below), not a live defect.

## High Priority Issues

### H-A. 36 of 40 content pages declare a `dateModified` older than their last actual edit

Not staleness — a **false freshness signal, pointing the wrong way.**

Every one of those 36 files was edited on **2026-08-31** — the day 153 unsourceable claims were stripped (`47818d6`) and the FATF/STR facts corrected (`47610ae`). All 36 still declare `updatedAt` of **2026-03-25** or **2026-06-18**.

```
knowledge/cambodia-fintech-regulation.ts   declares 2026-03-25   edited 2026-08-31
insights/bakong-adoption-metrics.ts        declares 2026-03-25   edited 2026-08-31
learn/cambodia-aml-requirements.ts         declares 2026-03-25   edited 2026-08-31
… 33 more
```

The pages whose **facts were corrected** are the ones telling AI systems nothing has changed since March. A model holding the pre-correction version has no modification signal prompting a re-crawl, so the corrected text may never displace it. Freshness is also a direct ranking input.

**Fix:** set `updatedAt` to the real edit date on all 36. One commit, one deploy. Then add it to the `check:llms` guard — this is precisely the class of drift that guard exists to catch, and it is a mechanical comparison against `git log`.

### H-B. `/insights` publishes 573 numeric figures with zero citations

| Page | Figures | Real outbound citations |
|---|---|---|
| `sme-digital-readiness` | 118 | 0 |
| `bakong-adoption-metrics` | 108 | 0 |

The only external `href` on each page is the Google Analytics script tag. `bakong-adoption-metrics` carries **no inline source attribution at all** — not even unlinked prose. `sme-digital-readiness` at least names the World Bank and ADB in prose, unlinked.

This is the site's own stated standard turned against its weakest section. `CLAUDE.md` holds `/regulatory` to an absolute evidence bar on the reasoning that *"correct-but-unsourced and wrong-but-confident are indistinguishable from inside a document"* — and `/insights` is 573 figures of exactly that, on the same domain, under the same brand. A reader who checks one figure and cannot trace it discounts the whole corpus, `/regulatory` included.

**Fix (choose one, do not leave it as-is):** source the figures, or move the section behind a dated "analysis, not data" notice rendered by the layout (the `ScenarioNotice` pattern), or retire it. Given memory records ~955 prose figures still open corpus-wide, this is the concentrated worst case and the right place to start.

## Opportunities — not defects

**`/regulatory` is a publishable asset, not just a reference section.** 17 instruments at a documented evidence bar is the raw material for exactly the contributor-channel articles the competitor set uses to hold third-party authority pages. The hard part — fetching and verifying primaries — is already done.

**Khmer remains uncontested.** ADR-007's English-default decision is correct and the absence of `hreflang` is consistent with it, so this is not a defect. But Khmer-language queries about Bakong, CamInvoice and DASP licensing still return nothing from this domain, and every competitor named in this audit publishes English only. `titleKh` on the 17 instruments — transcription from a fetched source, which `regulatory/[slug]/page.tsx` already renders and no instrument populates — is the smallest honest first move.

**The sensor network is still unpublished primary data.** Six sensors produce original observations about Cambodian regulatory and market activity. AI systems disproportionately cite original data, and `/insights` currently fills that slot with 573 unsourced figures. AI-crawler access tested by issuing live requests under the GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot user agents. `updatedAt` drift measured by comparing each content file's declared date against `git log -1` for that file. Brand and platform findings from an independent search sweep using WebSearch, direct HTTP fetch, the Wikidata API and the GitHub API, briefed on the Aston Hill guardrail before starting.
